SURVIVOR’S HANDBOOK / 01

Stay on your feet.

Open City keeps one connected town with streets, parks, a canal and bridges. Easy driving turns the car toward your movement keys or left stick and brakes when released. Touch always uses this handling, with separate brake and reverse buttons. Steering/pedals is available in Settings: W/S or R2/L2 accelerate and reverse, A/D or the left stick steer. Stop, then press V or Triangle to exit. Choose assisted driving in Settings. Press M for the map. Discover landmarks for supplies. Find the three optional radio encounters on the atlas: secure a supply convoy, restart a relay and hunt an enforcer for distinct rewards. Choose Biome Campaign for the eight-region route. Every run generates a new map: streets, cover, fuel clusters and buildings. Fight across eight biomes and thirty-two waves. Choose a perk after each wave. Defeat eight bosses, then hold the extraction beacon for four seconds. Earn scrap even when you die and spend it on permanent upgrades at base camp.

YOUR CHARACTER · Open Character from the title screen or Character & Journal in pause. Spend permanent level points on five attributes and twelve skills; equip head, chest and leg armour and choose a dye. City traders sell tiered gear, offer contracts and reveal hidden bosses. Find them on the atlas; approach on foot and press V, Triangle or TALK / TRADE. Levels, skills and discovered gear survive death. Crowns and quest progress reset each expedition. All progress is saved in this browser.

FEMelee & grenades

Equip a melee weapon in the arsenal for three-hit combos. F swings your equipped melee weapon. Hold attack for a three-hit sequence, or tap near the end of recovery to chain the next cut. Dodge cancels an unfinished swing. Blades replace both guns and grant faster movement, quicker dodges and combo recovery. Grenades land at your cursor, or your target on touch. Blast facades repeatedly to break them apart.

1 – 0BRSwitch & reload

Equip up to two firearms firing together. Thirteen types, including katana, twin swords, axe, spear and hammer. Choose fragmentation, fire, frost or shock throwables in the arsenal. All share ordnance supplies. Reserve ammunition is unlimited. Precision weapons can explode heads; heavy hits can sever limbs and leave crawlers.

ESCPTake a breath

Pause anytime. The game also pauses when you switch away.

GAMEPAD · Left stick moves, right stick aims. L2 fires your left weapon; R3 fires your right weapon (switch to L2/R2 in Settings). Cross dodges, Circle brakes a vehicle, Square reloads, Triangle enters/exits a car. L1 throws grenades, R1 cycles the right-hand weapon, D-pad Up opens the arsenal, L3 toggles auto fire, Create / touchpad opens the map, Options pauses. Reassign combat buttons in Settings. In menus use D-pad, Cross to select and Circle to return. On the atlas: right stick moves the crosshair, left stick pans, triggers zoom, Cross marks, Square clears and R3 recentres.

ON TOUCHSCREENS · Left thumb moves; right thumb aims and attacks. Tap DODGE or BOMB above the right stick, SWAP or RELOAD above the left. AUTO toggles automatic targeting. Adjust floating/fixed sticks, size, opacity and aim assist in Settings. Drag the map to pan, pinch to zoom and tap to mark. Pick up marked supplies for armour, overdrive, freeze, double score and more. Shoot crates for supplies. Red barrels and vehicles explode—keep your distance.
