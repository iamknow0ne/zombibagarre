TAKE THE HIGH ROAD.

Jump onto the cargo, then grapple to three rooftops. Tap grapple again to release the cable. Move in the air to adjust your landing.

Marked anchors are solid attachment points. A blocked or distant anchor cannot pull you through a building. Falling returns you to the street; retry returns you to your last rooftop.

This is a movement prototype. City combat, roof quests and enemy climbing are not enabled here.
